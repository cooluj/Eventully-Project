About

Why Eventully exists

UW has {{ stats.total_clubs|commas }} registered student organizations. That's the good news. The bad news is that finding yours means scrolling an alphabetical list longer than a CSE lecture. Most students give up somewhere around the D's.

Eventully asks you three questions, what you're into, what you study, and how much time you actually have, then scores every club in the directory against your answers. You get a ranked shortlist with the reasoning shown, not a black box. And when a club posts an event, the people who care about it hear about it.

For club officers

Every registered UW club already has a page here. If you run one, claim it: rewrite the description, add your links and meeting times, and post events that students can register for, with live capacity counts, calendar export, member messaging, and an attendee list only you can see. Claims are reviewed by hand to keep listings legitimate.

The fine print

  • Registration requires a .edu email address.
  • The directory is built from UW's public list of registered student organizations.
  • Eventully is an independent student project, not officially affiliated with the University of Washington.
  • Something wrong with a listing, or a claim to dispute? Reach out and we'll fix it.

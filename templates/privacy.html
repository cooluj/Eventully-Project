Privacy policy

Last updated July 3, 2026

What Eventully collects

We store the information needed to run the product: your name, email, password hash, club preferences, club memberships, saved clubs, event registrations, officer claims, club messages, and officer-created listing and event content.

Who can see what

Club officers can see attendee names and emails for events they manage, and the members of their club. Club messages are visible to members of that club, approved club officers, and site admins for support and safety.

How we use it

We use your information to recommend clubs, run registrations, review officer claims, deliver account emails and the notifications you've opted into, and help clubs communicate with their members. We do not sell student data and we run no advertising or third-party tracking.

Account and data requests

You can delete your account from Settings at any time; it removes your profile, memberships, registrations, and saved clubs. Contact the Eventully admin for a correction or help with a club ownership dispute. Deleted messages may be retained for safety review but are hidden from normal views.
